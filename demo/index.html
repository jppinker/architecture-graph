How to read the graphs

Confirmed dependencies

Solid relations backed by exact repository-relative source evidence. These are the only Batch-10 code relations traversed by impact analysis.

Context-only declarations

Useful declarations that do not prove runtime dependency or traffic. They remain visible but are excluded from impact paths.

Unsupported or unresolved

Patterns whose target or semantics cannot be proven by the bounded rules. They never become graph edges, and absence of an edge is not proof of safety.
